Local SEO & Google Maps06

What is slowing you down

People search in their own words — “salon near me”, “best kottu Negombo” — and your pages use wording only you would search for. The business that gets found is not always the best one, just the clearest one.

What we build

Your business set up for local search: a complete Google profile, pages structured in plain searchable language, and fast pages that keep the visitor once they land.

What you get

More chances to appear when nearby customers search, with each page built to turn that visit into an enquiry instead of a bounce.

What gets built

  • Google Business Profile setup and consistency check
  • Titles, headings, and service copy written around how locals search
  • Location and service pages structured for clarity
  • Internal links pointing the right pages to the right searches
  • Speed and mobile checks so landing pages load fast
  • A simple monthly check-in on what is improving

No ranking promises: local search depends on competition, reviews, and distance. We do the work properly and report what changes — we do not sell positions.
