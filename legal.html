OmniFlow Digital ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website or contact us, how we use it, and the choices you have.

How We Use Your Information

  • To comply with applicable legal, accounting, or regulatory obligations.

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal details to third parties.

Third-Party Services We Use

Depending on how you interact with us, your enquiry may pass through trusted service providers sole-purpose bound to deliver that communication:

  • Static hosting & CDN: Our hosting provider processes standard server logs (such as IP address and request timestamp) to keep the site fast and secure.

We keep enquiry records only as long as needed to assist you, maintain our business relationship, or meet legal requirements. You can ask us at any time to view, update, or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing hello@yourdomain.com.

3. Cookie & Storage Policy Browser Storage

We keep our website lightweight and respectful of your privacy. By default, this site does not set advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

What We Store in Your Browser

  • Theme preference (localStorage key omni-theme): Remembers whether you selected dark mode or light mode so the site doesn't flash or reset on your next visit. This stays entirely in your browser and is never sent to a server.
  • Optional analytics cookies (only if enabled): If Google Analytics or a privacy-friendly analytics script is enabled at launch, it may set first-party cookies (such as _ga) to distinguish unique visits and measure which pages are most helpful.

Managing Cookies & Local Storage

You can clear cookies and site data at any time in your browser settings, or use private/incognito browsing. Clearing site storage simply resets your dark/light theme preference back to your device default.
