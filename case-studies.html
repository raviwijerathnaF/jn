Clinic appointment journeyBlueprint

Problem

Appointment requests can arrive across calls, social messages, and walk-ins, making it difficult to keep availability and follow-up in one place.

Solution Architecture

A mobile-first clinic page connects services and booking requests to an agreed WhatsApp workflow, with clear staff handoff and optional calendar integration.

Outcome

Proposed outcome: a clearer appointment journey and a repeatable process for handling requests. This is not a report of live-client results.

What gets built

  • Mobile-first clinic and service information pages
  • Appointment request form with clear next steps
  • WhatsApp enquiry routing and confirmation copy
  • Optional appointment reminders and calendar handoff
  • Google Business Profile links and contact actions
  • Staff handover notes and an agreed launch checklist

Retail stock and enquiry flowBlueprint

Problem

When product availability, checkout, and customer questions use separate processes, staff may need to reconcile information manually.

Solution Architecture

A scoped POS and inventory setup connects product records, barcode workflows, stock alerts, and an optional WhatsApp availability or reservation path.

Outcome

Proposed outcome: a documented stock workflow that connects the chosen checkout and customer enquiry tools, with no performance results implied.

What gets built

  • Product catalogue and agreed stock structure
  • POS configuration for the selected counters or locations
  • Barcode and inventory update workflow
  • Low-stock alert rules based on agreed thresholds
  • Optional WhatsApp availability and reservation flow
  • Role-based access, staff handover, and operating notes

Hospitality discovery-to-booking flowBlueprint

Problem

Guests may find a menu or venue on one channel and then need to switch apps or make a call to ask about bookings and orders.

Solution Architecture

A mobile menu and local discovery setup leads into an agreed WhatsApp booking or order workflow, with staff confirmation and clear operating hours.

Outcome

Proposed outcome: a straightforward route from venue information to an enquiry. This blueprint contains no live-client performance claims.

What gets built

  • Mobile-friendly venue and digital menu pages
  • Table enquiry or direct-order call to action
  • WhatsApp booking or order intake workflow
  • Google Business Profile menu and contact links
  • Operating-hours, location, and service information
  • Staff handover guide and launch checklist
